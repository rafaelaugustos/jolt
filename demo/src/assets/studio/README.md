# Motion Studio artwork

`journey.svg` is original vector artwork drawn in code for the coordinated timeline.

`cove.png` and `dunes.png` are generated editorial illustrations, created with the built-in image generation tool for the showcase. They are local sample artwork, not photographs of an identified location. The original generated images were resized to a 1200-pixel long edge for the app. The isolated image-effect test fixtures remain unchanged.

Prompts:

- Cove: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: an editorial photograph used inside a native mobile image-effects and photo-zoom demo. Create a stunning portrait-format aerial photograph of a quiet Mediterranean cove: sculptural cream limestone cliffs framing deep emerald and jade water, a narrow sunlit ochre road curving around the rocky headland, tiny white foam ribbons at the shore. Restrained warm film color grading, late-afternoon angled sunlight, fine realistic rock texture and clear water. Sophisticated travel magazine art direction, visually rich at small phone size and detailed when zoomed in. Full bleed photograph only, no typography, no logos, no borders, no UI. Portrait 2:3 composition.”
- Dunes: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: second editorial photograph for a native mobile gallery with zoom and dissolve transitions. Portrait full-bleed travel photograph of sculptural terracotta sandstone dunes in the desert at blue hour. Deep plum shadows, softly lit warm peach dune crests, a small pale moon in a muted lavender sky, gentle S-shaped dune ridge leading into the distance. Minimal, meditative, high-end analog travel magazine photography. Rich natural sand texture visible at zoom, subtle film grain, realistic light. No text, no logo, no frame, no UI. Portrait 2:3.”
